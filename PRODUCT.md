# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and technical collaborators reviewing Bryan Widjaja's work. Visitors need to understand his contribution, inspect real project evidence, and find his resume and contact details. The site must remain usable on phones, modest hardware, and slower connections.

## Product Purpose

A personal developer portfolio connecting machine learning research, software projects, and programming teaching. The active reference implementation lives in `ref-app/`; the owner's root-level hand-build remains paused. `CONTEXT.md`, the current source, and `notes/plan/45-ink-approved.md` supply the existing decisions.

## Brand Personality

Painted, patient, precise. Chinese ink painting and calligraphy inspire the established identity. The primary historical portfolio reference is Darryl Effendi's site; borrow clarity and motion intent without copying its identity.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS layouts, decorative glass panels, fake project screens, invented credentials, exaggerated scientific claims, decorative Chinese characters, and cultural motifs misrepresented as Chinese. Paper texture must not obscure text or evidence.

## Design Principles

- Let real project artifacts and the owner's contribution establish credibility.
- Preserve the established ink-and-paper identity when repairing presentation.
- Fit media to its evidence: project text, diagrams, controls, and labels must remain complete.
- Use motion selectively and measure its cost on the existing application.
- Make biography, project navigation, resume access, and contact easy to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the existing WCAG AA contrast target, visible keyboard focus, semantic text alternatives, keyboard equivalents for pointer interactions, native touch scrolling, and reduced-motion static states. Image viewers must preserve focus, expose usable controls on short screens, and allow visitors to reach every item without moving the background page.
